# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

An even mix of technical peers, potential collaborators, clients, and employers exploring Brian Kareithi's work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that introduces Brian, explains his technical background, and lets visitors explore shipped projects, skills, experiments, and ways of working.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents the work and engineering reasoning directly, without making hiring the sole purpose of the experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site includes project case studies, profile and process pages, a resume, contact details, a homelab, troubleshooting content, minigames, a command palette, and responsive navigation. Preserve existing route behavior and accessibility.

## Brand Commitments

Use grape and wine red as the color identity. The user requests a subtle, impressive presentation with no gradients and no grid pattern in the footer.

## Evidence on Hand

Project screenshots, a portrait, resume PDF, project documentation, skills data, and testimonial content are present in the repository. Keep content factual and do not invent proof points.
